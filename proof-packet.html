Primary work

Sarrus + FluxCell

Use Sarrus as the core technical work: programmable soft robotic surfaces and bodies. The mechanism page is public at sarrus.aolabs.io/mechanism.html. Use FluxCell as an actuation concept only where the public record supports it.

  • Mechanism page: geometry, compliant link and flexural-joint path, pressure-driven expansion, constraint/bias, and module assembly.
  • Measurement table: pressure range, expansion ratio, leg angle, force, stiffness, hysteresis status, dome height, double-dome resolution, and iteration boundary.
  • Visual record: linkage geometry, module parts, module biasing, pressure-dependent force/stiffness figures, and object-manipulation motion.
  • Unresolved data: digitized force, stiffness, and hysteresis curves with uncertainty and test conditions.
Motion

Motion and interaction

The strongest WDI version of the work is not only a soft robotics mechanism. It is a physical interaction system: soft, programmable shape or motion that can make an object feel alive, responsive, surprising, or believable.

  • What the guest sees: a physical surface or body changes shape with readable intent.
  • What the mechanism shows: repeatable soft motion with a clear actuation path.
  • What the review checks: whether the page communicates technical credibility and physical interaction in under two minutes.
